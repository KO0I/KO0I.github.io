## Hi! I'm Amber!


I am interested in amateur radio, particularly in homebrewing
      equipment. I hold an Extra License with the
      callsign KO0I, but I've only recently found the space to build my HF
      antenna. I have been
      playing with RepRap machines for many years (since 2010) and presently own my best
      approximation of a Prusa i3, I use it to print all kinds of things. I
      enjoy gardening, zero-waste, tinkering, trading, being outdoors, and bicycling.
     
I enjoy making hardware with Verilog/SVA, but I haven’t been able to mess with my SnickerDoodle or other FPGA stuff for fun due to need of variety between my hobbies and work.
      
Recently I have been getting into developing a flying/space exploration game in C set in a hopeful post-scarcity society. It uses pixel art in X Pixmap (XPM) format and 2.5D graphics. It’s letting me grow my skills with openMP and learn shaders (well, I am barely absorbing shaders), which means I tend to favor CPU-heavy  graphics. Video games are very challenging because they are so very multidisciplinary.
